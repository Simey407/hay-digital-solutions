PRIVACY NOTICE

How we handle information.

Hay Digital Solutions processes information needed to respond to enquiries, provide purchased services, administer payments and maintain appropriate business records.

For a Payment Health Check, this can include business contact details and the business documents and payment-process information you choose to provide. We ask customers to minimise personal data and not submit passwords, payment-card data, full bank statements, health information, HR data or other information unrelated to the review.

Information is used only where necessary to administer or provide the service, meet applicable legal obligations, or pursue legitimate business purposes where appropriate. Access is restricted and customer material is not intended to be made public.

Payment processing is provided separately by Stripe. Intake and file-submission arrangements may use a specialist form provider; relevant provider details and retention arrangements will be confirmed before live customer document collection begins.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected and applicable record-keeping obligations. More specific operational retention periods will be published once the live intake and storage process has completed testing.

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact hello@haydigitalsolutions.co.uk.
