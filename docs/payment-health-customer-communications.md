# Payment Health Check — Customer Communications Pack

Owner: Hay Digital Solutions  
Service: Payment Health Check  
Price: £199 one-off  
Turnaround: normally 3 working days after all reasonably required information has been received.

## Operating rules

- Use a customer reference in the format PHC-0001.
- Do not start the three-working-day turnaround clock until the case is READY FOR REVIEW.
- Make one consolidated request for missing information where practical.
- Never ask customers to send passwords, card details, full bank statements, HR records, health information or unrelated personal information.
- Customers should redact personal information that is not relevant to the review.
- Do not promise recovery of debts, faster payment, legal outcomes or a particular financial saving.
- The service is a commercial payment-process review, not legal, accounting, tax or regulated financial advice, debt collection or a credit-reference service.

## Internal case-status sequence

**PAID → INTAKE OUTSTANDING → INTAKE RECEIVED → READY FOR REVIEW → REVIEWING → QA → DELIVERED → FOLLOW-UP**

Do not move a case to READY FOR REVIEW merely because a form has been submitted. Check that the material is sufficient for a meaningful evidence-based review first.

## File naming

- Customer folder: `PHC-0001_Business-Name`
- Original uploads: retain original filenames inside the case folder.
- Working evidence ledger: `PHC-0001_Evidence-Ledger`
- Final report: `PHC-0001_Business-Name_Payment-Health-Check.pdf`
- Delivery record: `PHC-0001_Delivery-Record`

## Pre-delivery communication QA

Before sending the report, confirm:

1. customer/business and reference are correct;
2. the final report passed the Payment Health QA checklist;
3. Critical and High findings received human review;
4. uncertain or missing evidence is labelled correctly;
5. no legal/accounting/tax/regulated-financial conclusion has slipped into the wording;
6. attachment filename is correct;
7. recipient address is correct; and
8. the delivery date is recorded.

This pack is an internal operating document. Customer-facing messages should be adapted only where the individual case genuinely requires it.
