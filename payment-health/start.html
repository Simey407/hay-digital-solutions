Payment received.
Now show us the real process.

To begin your review, email the information below to hello@haydigitalsolutions.co.uk. Your normal 3-working-day turnaround begins once we have the information reasonably required to carry out the review.

Start by email

1 · BUSINESS CONTEXT

Tell us how payment normally works.

In the body of your email, include:

Business name and what you do
Approximate number of B2B invoices raised per month
Typical invoice value or range
Normal payment terms
Who raises invoices and who follows up payment
Accounting/invoicing system used, if relevant
The main payment problem you want this review to investigate

2 · EVIDENCE

Attach a small, representative evidence pack.

Where available, send:

One representative invoice
Your current payment terms, accepted quote or relevant contract wording
An anonymised aged-debt report or recent invoice/payment history
Your current chasing process or example reminder emails
One or two examples of invoices that were paid late, where useful

You do not need to send everything your business holds. A focused sample is more useful than a large data dump.

3 · DATA MINIMISATION

Please remove anything we do not need.

Redact or omit unrelated personal information. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, full bank statements, health information, HR records, authentication credentials or other sensitive information unrelated to the payment process.

If a document contains customer names or personal contact details that are not necessary to understand the process, anonymise them before sending it.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

We check the evidence before starting the clock.

We will confirm whether the evidence is sufficient for the review. If one important item is missing, we will ask for it rather than make unsupported assumptions. The report will distinguish evidence-backed findings from limitations or areas where the available evidence is insufficient.

The Payment Health Check remains a commercial operational review. It is not legal advice, accounting or tax advice, regulated financial advice, debt collection, credit referencing or a legal determination.
